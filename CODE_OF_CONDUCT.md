# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project community. Participation should be respectful regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, appearance, nationality, ethnicity, religion, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age, or other protected characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and professional.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwanted behavior.
- Make room for different levels of experience and communicate in good faith.
- Protect confidential reports and personal information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm where possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, stalking, doxxing, publishing private information, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable. Abusive conduct is prohibited in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository activity, issue and review discussions, project events, and other spaces officially associated with the project. It also applies when a participant publicly represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Conduct reports should be handled privately and impartially. A permanent private conduct-reporting channel has not yet been published; until it is, request private maintainer contact in a public issue without including names, evidence, or sensitive details.

Maintainers may use clarification, a warning, temporary limits, removal from project spaces, or a permanent ban depending on impact and pattern. They must minimize disclosure, avoid conflicts of interest, prohibit retaliation, and document decisions privately. A maintainer named in a report must not handle that report.

Before public community launch, the project must publish a durable private reporting channel and name the enforcement team. Those are explicit maintainer decisions, not safe values for an implementer to invent.
