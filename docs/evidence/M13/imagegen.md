# ImageGen provenance

The M13 social-preview artwork was generated once with OpenAI's built-in ImageGen and visually
reviewed before inclusion as `frontend/src/app/opengraph-image.png` (1536 x 1024 PNG). No external
image search or third-party asset was used.

## Prompt

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: Open Graph social preview background for an open-source,
> API-first personal website and CMS. Primary request: Create a refined editorial abstract image
> that communicates a personal body of work organized like a precise signal ledger. Scene/backdrop:
> Warm off-white paper field with a dark navy architectural grid, fine registry lines, a few
> terracotta signal markers, and restrained pale blue data traces. Style/medium: Premium editorial
> graphic design, modernist print composition, tactile paper grain, crisp geometric structure,
> understated and trustworthy. Composition/framing: Wide 3:2 landscape composition with generous
> negative space through the center-left; balanced at thumbnail size; no UI screenshot.
> Lighting/mood: Calm, rigorous, human, quietly confident. Color palette: Off-white, ink navy,
> muted terracotta, pale blue-gray. Constraints: No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no
> faces, no devices, no gradients that harm contrast, no watermark. Suitable for cropping to
> 1200x630.
