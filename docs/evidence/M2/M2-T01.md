## Contract contents

- Success/error envelopes and `X-Request-ID` correlation.
- Stable safe error/status catalog including validation, authentication, authorization, hidden
  not-found, conflict, precondition, rate limit, internal failure, and dependency failure.
- Page-number pagination with defaults 1/20, maximum 100, exact totals, and beyond-last behavior.
- Allow-listed filters/sorts with deterministic `id` tie-breaking and duplicate rejection.
- RFC 3339 UTC timestamps, calendar dates, `snake_case`, and opaque UUID identifiers.
- Public, administrator-session, and future token-shaped actor interfaces with use-case
  authorization boundaries.
- Integer-version ETag/`If-Match` primitives and `428`/`409` mappings.
- Actor+route+key idempotency with 24-hour retention, safe replay, payload conflict, and concurrent
  exactly-once admission.
